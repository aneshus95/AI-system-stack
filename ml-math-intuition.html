Why the math looks like this

In Chapter 2 we met the tools. Now let's understand why each one has the form it does.

We won't just state rules. For each idea we'll start with an everyday situation, see the problem it solves, work out why the rule has to look the way it does, and connect it back to the basics before it.

The thread running through everything. Each idea exists because the one before it wasn't enough. Variables let one rule cover many cases. Equations let us solve for unknowns. Functions give the rule a direction. Graphs let us see it, slope measures it, and the derivative extends slope to curves. Vectors hold many inputs, dot products combine them, gradients tell us how to improve, and the chain rule carries that through a whole model.
